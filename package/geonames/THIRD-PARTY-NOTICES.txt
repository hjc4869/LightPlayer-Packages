LightStudio.GeoNames contains data derived from GeoNames.

Attribution: GeoNames, https://www.geonames.org/
Source: https://download.geonames.org/export/dump/
License: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0)
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

The dataset is filtered to populated places in cities1000 and their available
administrative regions, with country information and selected alternate names.
It is encoded as a compact binary database with quantized coordinates, shared
strings, a parent hierarchy, and a balanced spherical search index.

GeoNames provides its data "as is", without warranty or any representation of
accuracy, timeliness, or completeness. GeoNames does not endorse this package.

Applications redistributing this data must retain attribution, identify their
changes, and provide the license link. See build-info.json for the input URLs
and SHA-256 checksums of the source snapshot used to create this database.
